Privacy

Erlang Desk is built so that your contact-centre data stays on your device.

Your planning data

Contact history you import, settings and hiring plans are processed in your web browser and stored in your browser's local storage. They are not sent to our servers. Clearing your browser data removes them; use Export workspace to keep a copy.

Billing

If you buy a subscription, payment is handled by our payment provider (Stripe). We receive your name, email address and subscription status, never your card details.

Analytics

We may collect anonymous page-view statistics to improve the product. These never include the contents of your workspace.

Contact

Questions about privacy can be sent to the contact address on our website.
