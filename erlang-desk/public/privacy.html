Privacy notice

Draft for legal review before launch.

Your planning data stays on your device

Contact history, forecasts, settings and hiring plans are processed in your web browser and stored in its local storage. They are never sent to our servers. Clearing your browser data removes them; use Export workspace to keep a copy.

What we hold about you

If you create an account we process your email address to send sign-in links and to match you to your subscription. We do not use passwords. Sessions are kept in a secure, HttpOnly cookie that expires after seven days.

Payments

Subscriptions are processed by Stripe. Stripe holds your billing name, address and payment details; we never see or store card numbers.

Processors

  • Vercel — hosting
  • Stripe — billing
  • Resend — sign-in emails

Cookies

We set only strictly necessary cookies: a short-lived sign-in cookie and a session cookie. No advertising or tracking cookies.

Your rights

You can ask for a copy of the data we hold about you, or for it to be deleted, by contacting us at the address on our website. You can cancel your subscription at any time from the billing page.
